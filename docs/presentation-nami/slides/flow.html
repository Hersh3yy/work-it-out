The journey of one message

Six stops, and it all happens in about one second.

Telegram

you send it

Feetness

gets it

Sorter

what is it?

Reader

finds numbers

Database

saves it

Reply

back to you

These two use AI. Everything else is normal code.

The app lives on a rented computer in a data centre (a "server"). Your phone talks to it over the internet.

Walk the boxes left to right as they pop in. The sorter and the reader are the only two AI steps. Everything else is ordinary code, which is fast and never makes things up.
