Gym apps are boring to fill in

The old way

Open the app, tap 20 buttons, type every set by hand.

Sweaty hands, tiny buttons, and by the third exercise you give up.

The Feetness way

Just text it, like you would text a friend.

bench 3x8 80

The app works out the rest: which exercise, how many sets, how heavy.

Uncle Hiren wants to lose fat and get stronger. Logging in normal apps is so annoying that people stop. So: text it, and let the app do the boring part.
