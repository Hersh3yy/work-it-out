How you get in

Like a party with a guest list. No invite, no entry.

1

Uncle Hiren makes a secret link

It works once, and only for 7 days.

2

You tap it and press Start

Telegram opens a chat with the bot.

3

The bot knows it is you

From now on, your texts go into your log.

A stranger who finds the bot gets one polite "this is a private beta" reply a day, and nothing else.

Each link is used once and expires after 7 days, so a link that leaks is useless quickly. The app only stores a scrambled version of the code. Ask Nami: why should a link work only once?
