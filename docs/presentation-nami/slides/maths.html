The AI types. The maths thinks.

AI is great at reading messy messages, but it can make up numbers. So every number the app shows is worked out with plain maths.

You lift

80 kg × 8

80 × (1 + 8 ÷ 30)

≈ 101 kg

That is a guess of the heaviest weight you could lift once. Coaches have used this formula for decades. It never gets tired and never invents anything.

This is the Epley formula for an estimated one-rep max. Let Nami work it out: 8 divided by 30 is about 0.27, plus 1 is 1.27, times 80 is about 101. The point: the AI only turns text into numbers; maths does the thinking.
