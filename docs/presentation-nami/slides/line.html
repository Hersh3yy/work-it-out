Plan 1: ask once, not twice

Today a message can use two questions: sort it, then read it. One combined question halves the waiting.

Plan 2: a waiting line

Messages wait in line (a queue) instead of failing. The reply comes a bit later, and nothing gets lost.

This is the next thing to build, not done yet. Like a ride at a theme park that only takes a few people per minute: better to wait in line than be sent home. Ask Nami: would you rather wait 10 seconds or have to send it again?
