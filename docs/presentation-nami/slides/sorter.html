Stop 3: the sorter

Like sorting mail into five boxes before reading it. What do you mean with this message?

Workout

"ran 5k in 28 min"

About me

"weighed 103 this morning"

Feeling

"my left knee hurts"

Question

"what should I train today?"

Nonsense

"rainbow butterfly"

Only workouts and body weights go on to the reader. A question goes to a coach later. "rainbow butterfly" gets a friendly "huh?".
