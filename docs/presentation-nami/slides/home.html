Where the app lives

Not on the laptop. On a rented computer in a data centre that never sleeps. Programmers call it a server.

One little office building with four helpers inside

The mail room

keeps asking Telegram: any new messages?

The front desk

lets in visitors from the web, later the app

The helper

does jobs in the background, like adding up your streak

The clock

wakes up every minute to check for chores

The filing cabinet

every saved workout, next door (the database)

The app runs on a rented computer managed with a tool called Coolify. Inside one box live four helpers: the Telegram listener, the web front desk, a background worker and a clock. Ask Nami: why is it better than running it on the laptop? (The laptop sleeps and closes; the server does not.)
