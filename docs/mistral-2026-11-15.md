# The coach and the profile on Mistral, 15 November 2026

Hiren wants the AI coach on an EU provider instead of Gemini Flash-Lite, and probably the profile writer too. This file holds the pick, the prices, what changed in code, what is not verified yet, and his steps in Coolify.

## The pick

Mistral Large 4, API id `mistral-large-4-0`, for the coach (questions and the morning message) and for the profile, on Mistral's EU endpoint `https://api.eu.mistral.ai/v1`.

Mistral Small 4 (`mistral-small-2603`) is the cheaper choice for the profile if Large 4 costs more than it gives there: the profile is short structured notes, not advice.

The library's own defaults are out of date: its default `mistral-large-2512` is Mistral Large 3 and its "smartest" `mistral-medium-3-5` is Medium 3.5; Mistral marks both deprecated since 31 October 2026 with Large 4 as the replacement (docs.mistral.ai/getting-started/models, read 15 Nov). That is why the model is always named in the environment.

One coach answer, at the measured 4,000 tokens in and an assumed 500 out: about $0.0042 on Large 4 in the EU, against about $0.0014 on `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` today. Ten answers a day is about $1.30 a month.

## Regional inference

The "Regional inference" switch on the pricing page is a separate endpoint, not a setting on the account:

- `api.mistral.ai` is global. Mistral "does not commit to a specific inference location" for it.
- `api.eu.mistral.ai` runs inference in data centres in EU and EFTA countries; `api.us.mistral.ai` in the United States.
- Same model ids, same key. Each region serves only the models hosted there, so the model must be checked against the EU endpoint before it gets traffic.
- Control-plane data (account, keys, billing, usage analytics) may still be handled outside the region.
- Zero data retention is a separate control from the region.

So the gateway needs a `url`: `config/ai.php` now reads `MISTRAL_URL`, default the global `https://api.mistral.ai/v1`. Coolify sets it to the EU endpoint.

## Thinking and the coach's token caps

Large 4 and Small 4 reason only through a `reasoning_effort` parameter (docs.mistral.ai/studio/conversations/reasoning). The app does not send it. Large 4 accepts low, medium and high and refuses "none"; the page does not say what Large 4 does when the parameter is left out. Whether thinking tokens count against `max_tokens` is not on the pages read either.

So the coach's caps stay at 800, 1500 and 2000 (`config/coach.php`), and the DeepSeek-sized caps from the 14 November direction are not needed. The first real call settles it: if `ai_calls.reasoning_tokens` is above zero, or an answer comes back empty or cut off, the caps go up.

## What changed in code

- `config/ai.php`: the Mistral provider reads `MISTRAL_URL`; `prices` has `mistral-large-4-0` and `mistral-small-2603` at the EU rate.
- `.env.example`: the Mistral lines.
- `tests/Feature/Ai/MistralTest.php`: the real coach and profile adapters on the library's Mistral driver with the HTTP call faked. The coach call goes to the EU URL with the model and the depth's `max_tokens`, `ai_calls` records provider `mistral` and model `mistral-large-4-0`, and `UsageReport` prices it ($0.004235 for 4,000 in and 500 out). The profile call sends a `json_schema` response format and its notes are read back.

Nothing else changes: the coach and profile jobs already read `AI_CHAT_*` and `AI_PROFILE_*`, and `App\Ai\ModelChain` falls back to the log job's Gemini model when Mistral is overloaded, rate limited or unreachable.

## Not verified yet

- The ids above come from Mistral's docs; no `GET /v1/models` has been run, so it is not confirmed that `api.eu.mistral.ai` serves `mistral-large-4-0`.
- `ai_calls.model` stores the `model` field of Mistral's answer. If Mistral answers with another string than the id it was asked for, `ai:usage` lists that string as unpriced and its price must be added under that name.
- Whether Large 4 thinks without `reasoning_effort` (see above).
- Answer quality: nobody has compared Large 4 with flash-lite on Hiren's data.
- Mistral's data processing addendum and privacy policy were not read (the pages did not load for the reader). Mistral's commercial terms (legal.mistral.ai/terms/commercial-terms-of-service) say customer data and outputs are not used for training except through opt-in or opt-out settings, Labs or preview models, and that the DPA at legal.mistral.ai/terms/data-processing-addendum applies when Mistral processes as a processor, with no signature step mentioned. Check the training setting in Mistral's console when making the key.

## Privacy, plainly

With the coach and the profile on Mistral's EU endpoint, their inference stays in the EU. The one-message steps still go to Google: the classifier (what kind of message is this), the log reader and the injury reader all run on the log job's Gemini model, so every message Hiren sends to the bot, injury notes included, still reaches Google. A coach or profile call also goes to Google when Mistral fails and the chain falls back to the log job's model. Moving those steps is a separate task (audit 2026-11-14 item 7: split the `log` job first).

## Hiren's steps in Coolify

Do these after the branch is merged and deployed.

1. Make a key at console.mistral.ai (API keys). While there, check that the setting that allows Mistral to train on your data is off.
2. Open Coolify, then Projects, then Work it out, then production, then the app (the API).
3. Open Environment Variables. Add or change these, one per line:

```
MISTRAL_API_KEY=<the key>
MISTRAL_URL=https://api.eu.mistral.ai/v1
AI_CHAT_PROVIDER=mistral
AI_CHAT_MODEL=mistral-large-4-0
AI_PROFILE_PROVIDER=mistral
AI_PROFILE_MODEL=mistral-large-4-0
```

4. Remove these lines if they are there, they were for DeepSeek: `DEEPSEEK_API_KEY`, `COACH_TOKENS_QUICK`, `COACH_TOKENS_PLAN`, `COACH_TOKENS_EXPLAIN`, and an `AI_PROFILE_MODEL=gemini-...` line (replaced above). Leave `AI_LOG_PROVIDER`, `AI_LOG_MODEL`, `GEMINI_API_KEY` and `AI_FALLBACKS` alone: logging stays on Gemini.
5. Save, then press Redeploy and wait until the deployment says it finished.
6. Open the app's Terminal in Coolify and run, with your own email:

```
php artisan profile:show <email>
php artisan profile:rebuild <email> --full
php artisan profile:show <email>
```

The second `profile:show` should show freshly written notes.

7. Ask the bot a coach question in Telegram ("what should I train today?"), then in the same Terminal:

```
php artisan ai:usage --since=1d --by=model
```

The table should list `mistral-large-4-0` with a cost. If it lists `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` for the coach instead, Mistral failed and the chain fell back; if it lists another Mistral name as unpriced, tell the next session that name.
