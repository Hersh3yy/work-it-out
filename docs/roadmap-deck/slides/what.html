What it is

You text what you did. Three coaches text back.

Feetness turns plain words into real training data: sets, reps, weights, distance and time. It keeps your personal records and grows an RPG-style stat sheet with Strength, Stamina and Vitality.

No food tracking. Just training and body weight.
